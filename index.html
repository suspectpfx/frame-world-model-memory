Memory quality depends on the task performed by its reader. FRAME formulates memory serving through six distinct quantities: payload, fidelity, spatial granularity, physical layout, transform metadata and age. A consumer supplies a task-loss tolerance and a service deadline. Storage and access decisions then define a constrained frontier over bytes, latency and energy.

We evaluate two consequences of this formulation. A reversible permutation with unknown addressing disrupts a spatial reader; retaining the transform restores the original payload and fixed-reader output exactly. Lossy spatial aggregation has a different effect: its utility depends on the reader’s information requirements. CPU/GPU experiments further show that physical layout costs depend on whether the adapter materializes a tensor or is fused into the consumer. Giving both storage strategies the same adapter capability is essential to a fair comparison.

Experimental scope: cached-video readouts, synthetic spatial consumers, and 480 complete learned-policy episodes across fresh, stale, fixed-period refresh and inverse-recovered refresh controls on 40 paired initial states in each of three fixed LIBERO tasks. Comparative benefit from a calibrated FRAME selector remains unestablished.

02 · Method

A typed record, a task contract and a serving decision.

Definitions, implications and measurements are presented in one continuous derivation.

PROBLEM SETTING

A typed record meets a task contract at action time.

Each record carries a payload, representation tier, spatial granularity, physical layout, inverse metadata and capture time. The consumer declares its loss, tolerance, spatial contract, preferred layout and deadline.

SERVING RULE

Construct, verify, then compare feasible paths.

  1. Construct: direct return, inverse materialization, fused gather, imagination, or sensor acquisition.
  2. Verify: evaluate the accessed representation against the fixed consumer contract.
  3. Select: compare bytes, latency and energy only among candidates that satisfy task and deadline constraints.

05 · Experimental controls

Follow the information supplied to the reader.

The visible sequence and numerical tests use the same retained payload.

READOUT

Recovery is checked with a fixed reader

Holding reader weights fixed isolates the access intervention. Training a separate reader for every transformation would answer a different question.

SPATIAL INFORMATION

Aggregation can discard task-relevant detail

A many-to-one map cannot recover all inputs. Whether the discarded detail matters depends on the consumer’s target and tolerance.

SERVING

Both strategies receive the same adapter capability

The revised GPU comparison gives shared-canonical and permuted stores fused consumer adapters. This controls a material implementation difference.

06 · Validation program

Tie every claim to a rejection condition.

Completed evidence and remaining confirmatory gates appear together.

World-model memory. FRAME concerns how a record is represented, addressed, aged and served. Memory-augmented action-conditioned models such as Mem-World address the historical information used for persistent prediction. FRAME’s serving formulation is complementary; the current experiments do not establish closed-loop world-model task gains.

Tensor layout. Tensor Seeks Layout studies physical layout and conversion in compiler dataflow. FRAME additionally specifies consumer task utility, lossy spatial granularity and temporal access. Equal optimization of the access kernels remains necessary.

Physical-AI timing. ReflexBench provides latency-aware evaluation semantics. A future FRAME task claim requires actual model-selected actions, standard simulator success criteria and a physical timeline that accounts for model and sensing delay.

Limits of the serving study. The current matrix uses small-to-medium individual payloads, homogeneous batch mappings and interleaved consumers on one worker. Larger records, additional layout families, parallel tenants and calibrated practical-equivalence margins require separate validation. Full LIBERO, RoboTwin and dynamic eviction-baseline results are not reported.

Controlled action-world-model memory. Echo-Memory holds an action-to-video interface fixed while varying capacity, compression, readout and recurrence. FRAME targets a different systems boundary: shared-record consumer contracts, reversible layout metadata and adapters, lossy spatial granularity, age, temporal channel and measured service cost. The priority claim remains provisional until the declared confirmatory studies are complete.
